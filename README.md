# broforce-lite
a *Claude* demake of Broforce game for handhelds (This was made primarily for a Miyoo Mini+)

The goal was to have something fun like Broforce, when I don't have access to my computer. One player only.
If you don't know what Broforce is, well I strongly encourage you to have a look for this gem from "Free lives", edited by Devolver Digital, moreover it's really not expensive. In 2 words, that's a game about the good ol' time of 'merican movies like Rambo, that you can play multiplayer up to 4 on same screen. You control one of those guys, you free the ones that are prisoners, and bring peace using your weapons. When you free a bro, the one you are using changes for a new one, and you gain a life.

Don't hesitate reporting a bug or providing general feedback, suggestions; everything is welcome.

Musics come from free repos on the web, see sounds_licence.txt for reference.
